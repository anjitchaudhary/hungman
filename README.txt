How to run:
Open index.html in Chrome, Edge, Firefox, or Android Chrome.
Keep all folders together. The project is fully local and does not need an internet connection.

Question note:
The included bank is generated Class 12 NEB-style practice material. It is not presented as official NEB past-question material.

Core life system:
10 = top position
9 = one step down
8 = two steps down
...
1 = nine steps down
0 = falling/game over

Wrong answer: life - 1, smooth move down, rope becomes longer.
Correct answer: life + 1 up to 10, smooth move up, rope becomes shorter.
At 0: short pause, rope release, falling animation, river splash, result screen.
